Full-stack web app · Personal project

Anteater Study Spots

A UC Irvine campus companion: find a place to study, plan a focused session around what’s actually open, track Canvas due dates, and catch the next OCTA bus.

  • Lock-In Mode builds a study plan around open spots, with walking time and a food break, then runs a focus timer with ambient sound and streaks.
  • Canvas calendar sync shows every due date and breaks assignments into steps.
  • Campus map with live hours, weather, walking navigation, nearby food and a 3D fly-through of campus.
  • Transit: live OCTA bus positions, departures, nearest stop and a “leave by” suggestion.
